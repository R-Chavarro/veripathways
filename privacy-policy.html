Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Information you provide

VeriPathways may receive personal information that you voluntarily provide by email, such as your name, email address, academic interests, and details about your questions or goals.

How information is used

Information you provide is used to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, provide requested services, and communicate with you about your engagement with VeriPathways.

Information sharing

VeriPathways does not sell personal information. Information voluntarily provided by email is not shared with third parties except with service providers necessary to deliver and maintain email communications or when required by law.

Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect personal information. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or to meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@veripathways.com.
