Services

Package 1

College Conversation + Application Review

This package is recommended for students in grades 11 and 12 and includes approximately five to six sessions with students and their families. It begins with an initial consultation and a discussion of the student’s academic and professional goals, as well as the type of college experience they are seeking. This package includes guidance on college fit, including academic, social, and financial considerations, and a review of the student’s college list. Students and families also receive guidance on factors to consider when evaluating college options after admission. Finally, the package includes a comprehensive review of application materials, including essays, résumé, and other supporting documents. Students make their own revisions and retain full ownership of all final application content.

Package 2

Application Review

This package is recommended for students in grade 12 who are seeking application review only and includes approximately two to three sessions. This package includes a comprehensive review of the student’s application materials, including essays, résumé, and other supporting documents, with individualized feedback. Students make their own revisions and retain full ownership of all final application content.
