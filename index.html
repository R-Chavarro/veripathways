Common Questions

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Before scheduling a consultation, take some time to consider the questions you would like to discuss.

Who can benefit from VeriPathways?

Students and families preparing for college, navigating life on campus, considering research or experiential opportunities, or exploring PhD or other graduate programs can use consultations to think through their options.

Can I schedule additional consultation time?

Yes. Contact Rosa to discuss your needs and available scheduling options.

Will VeriPathways tell me what to choose?

VeriPathways can provide you with research, data, or perspective that can help you consider as many factors as possible in your planning for college and beyond.
