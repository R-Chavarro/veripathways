Common questions

Before you begin.

VeriPathways provides an independent perspective—not a sales pitch for a particular institution or program.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Start with your goals, the college, program, or path you are considering, expected costs, timeline, and any questions already on your mind. Rosa will confirm which additional information would be useful.

Who can benefit from VeriPathways?

Students and families preparing for college, navigating life on campus, considering research or experiential opportunities, or planning for graduate or PhD study can benefit from personalized guidance.

Can I schedule additional consultation time?

Yes. Contact Rosa to discuss your needs and available scheduling options.

Will Rosa tell me what to choose?

The goal is not to make the decision for you. Rosa provides research, data, perspective, and personalized guidance so you can understand the tradeoffs and make a confident decision that aligns with your goals, values, and aspirations.

Ready for a second opinion?

Tell Rosa where you are in your academic journey and which decision you are trying to make.
