About Me

Rosa Chavarro

Rosa is a higher-education professional with extensive experience guiding PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career professionals through consequential academic and career decisions.

At Columbia University, she has designed and led career-development programs serving more than 3,000 researchers and students annually and has provided one-on-one career counseling to more than 100 individuals each year. Her work brings together education planning, program evaluation, talent development, and a practical understanding of academic career paths.

Common questions

Before you begin.

Academic Pathways provides an independent perspective—not a sales pitch for a particular institution or program.

What should I submit for review?

Start with your goals, the program or path you are considering, expected costs, timeline, funding details, and any questions already on your mind. Rosa will confirm which additional materials are useful before beginning.

Is the follow-up consultation required?

No. Written findings can stand on their own. A consultation gives you an opportunity to discuss those findings and clarify next steps.

Can I purchase more time?

Yes. Contact Rosa to discuss additional consultation time and scheduling options.

Will Rosa tell me whether to stay or leave?

The goal is not to make the decision for you. The audit identifies tradeoffs, tests assumptions, and gives you a clearer basis for making an informed decision.

Ready for a second opinion?

Tell Rosa where you are in your academic journey and which decision you are trying to make.
