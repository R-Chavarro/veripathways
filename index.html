About Rosa

Rosa Chavarro

Rosa is a higher education professional with a decade of experience working across the higher education landscape. Through her work at a selective research institution and in student-centered higher education programs, Rosa has developed a unique perspective on how educational decisions influence long-term academic, professional, and personal outcomes. Drawing on professional experience and a research-driven approach, Rosa helps students and families navigate the complex decisions that shape the higher education journey.

Rosa helps students and their families think through the many factors that influence decisions about college, academic pathways, research opportunities, and graduate education. These choices are rarely simple—they require balancing academic, financial, personal, and career considerations that are unique to every student. Rosa does not tell students where they should go or what they should study. Instead, she provides the research, data, perspective, and personalized guidance that help students and their families think critically about their options, understand the tradeoffs, and make confident decisions that align with their goals, values, and aspirations.

Rosa believes that investing time in thoughtful decision-making today can save students and families significant time, money, stress, and uncertainty in the future. Every student follows a different path, and Rosa’s mission is to help each one maximize the value of their higher education by making intentional decisions that create lasting opportunities for academic, personal, and professional success.

Common questions

Before you begin.

VeriPathways provides an independent perspective—not a sales pitch for a particular institution or program.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Start with your goals, the college, program, or path you are considering, expected costs, timeline, and any questions already on your mind. Rosa will confirm which additional information would be useful.

Who can benefit from VeriPathways?

Students and families preparing for college, navigating life on campus, considering research or experiential opportunities, or planning for graduate or PhD study can benefit from personalized guidance.

Can I schedule additional consultation time?

Yes. Contact Rosa to discuss your needs and available scheduling options.

Will Rosa tell me what to choose?

The goal is not to make the decision for you. Rosa provides research, data, perspective, and personalized guidance so you can understand the tradeoffs and make a confident decision that aligns with your goals, values, and aspirations.
