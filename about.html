About

Rosa is a higher education leader with over a decade of experience in a variety of roles across educational institutions, including a national nonprofit organization and an R1 university. She holds a bachelor’s degree in history, a master’s degree in international educational development, and a certificate in teaching development.

Rosa has supported PhD trainees across disciplines as they explored and pursued their career goals, with particular focus in academic and faculty career paths. She has also contributed to college planning publications. While in graduate school, she served as a course assistant for a course on college student development, mentoring graduate students and supporting them with their writing and presentations.

Rosa is passionate about helping students and families navigate the complex decisions that shape their higher education journeys. She believes that investing time in thoughtful decision-making today can save students and families time, money, and stress in the future. Every student follows a different path, and Rosa’s mission is to help students consider their options and explore choices that align with their goals, values, and aspirations.
